More walk.
Less app.

Choosing a walk shouldn’t take longer than the walk. Hlid picks one you’ve never done, so all you have to do is go.

No set-up.
Just go.

Open it and today’s walk is already there. Change where you set off from, how long you want to drive or how long you want to walk, and it finds another.

Somewhere you’ve
never been

Not a list of 400 trails to scroll through, just one walk, picked for the day and its weather.

Google gets
you there

Hlid sends the route to Google Maps and always ends it at a free car park, so there’s no hunting for a space.

You’ll hardly
need a map

Once you’re walking, the map turns with you, so the way ahead is always up. It’s black and white, with the hills shaded the way Ordnance Survey used to. Glance at it if you’re unsure. Otherwise follow the signs and look up.

Keep the day
you had

No questions at the end, just one picture of your day: the drive and the walk as a single line. Its colour comes from the weather on the walk. Cold days come out blue, warm days gold, and cloud or rain makes it darker. Keep it as a wallpaper, then head home or let Hlid pick a pub nearby.

Nothing to unlock.
One price.

Offline maps, the wrong-way buzz and every walk are included. No account, no sign-up.
