Why I made
this app

For years my weekends were National Trust days. Drive out, look round a house, then a walk. Eventually I ran out of houses, and the walks became the point.

Planning them took longer than doing them. I asked an AI for ideas, then checked Google Maps, the parking, AllTrails and somewhere for lunch. By the time I decided, half the morning had gone.

On the walk, I spent too long looking at my phone: photos, reviews, stats. I wanted the opposite. A rough direction, a quiet path, and the feeling that I found it myself.

I'm a product designer, so I made the app I wanted. It picks one walk, sends me there and stays out of the way. At the end it gives me a picture of the day, which I keep.

These are some of those days, and what Hlid kept of them.
