How it works

Using Hlid

Where you are

Hlid starts from where you are. Somewhere else? Change it in one tap.

How long for

Pick a drive of up to two hours and a walk of one to four. That's it.

One walk

One circular walk from a free car park, picked for your times and the weather. Don't fancy it? Tap Another walk.

Drive

Tap Drive and Google Maps takes you to the car park.

Walk

The map turns with you. Go the wrong way and your phone buzzes.

Works offline

The map, your location and the wrong-way buzz all work without a connection.

Then the pub

Head home, or let Hlid find a pub nearby.

Keep the day

Each walk becomes a picture of your day, coloured by the weather. Keep it as a wallpaper.

One price

Pay once

No subscription, no premium tier, nothing to unlock. Every feature is in from day one.

No paid extras

Offline maps and the wrong-way buzz are paid extras in most walking apps. Here they're just part of it.

Where the walks come from

Real paths

Built from OpenStreetMap footpaths and Ordnance Survey height data. No routes copied from other apps.

Picked for a good day out

Ranked on views, hills, quiet and how easy the path is to follow. Busy spots drop down.

Honest times

Walk times use Naismith's rule: 5 km an hour, plus a minute for every 10 m of climb. Drive times don't include traffic.

What it can't see

Closures, mud or a full car park. Follow the signs on the ground.
