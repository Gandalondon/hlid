About

For years my weekends were National Trust days. Drive out of London, look round a house, then a walk. The drive into the country was half the joy. Eventually I ran out of houses, and the drive and the walk became the point.

Planning them took longer than doing them. I Googled for ideas, checked the route and the parking on Google Maps, read what people said about them in my walking app, then hunted for somewhere for lunch. By the time I decided, half the morning had gone.

What I wanted was the opposite. Stick a pin in a map and go. A drive I had never done, a walk I didn't know, and maybe a pub nearby if there's one. No star ratings, no checking every photo and menu before I leave.

I'm not against screens. I spend most of my week on one. I just didn't want one planning my day off.

I'm a product designer, so I made the app I wanted. It picks one walk, sends me there and stays out of the way. At the end it gives me a picture of the day, which I keep.

These are some of those days, and what Hlid kept of them.
