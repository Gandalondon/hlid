More walk. Less app.

Choosing a walk shouldn't take longer than the walk. Hlid picks one you've never done, so all you have to do is go.

No set-up. Just go.
Open it and today's walk is already there. Change where you set off from, how long you want to drive or how long you want to walk, and it finds another. One walk, not a list of 400 to scroll through, picked for the day and its weather.

Google gets you there
Hlid sends the drive to Google Maps and always ends it at a free car park at the start of the walk, so you don't have to work out where to park.

You'll hardly need a map
Once you're walking, the map turns with you, so the way ahead is always up. It's black and white, with the hills shaded the way Ordnance Survey used to. Wander well off the path and your phone buzzes once. Otherwise follow the signs and look up.

Keep the day you had
No questions at the end, just one picture of your day: the drive and the walk as a single line, coloured by the weather on the walk. Keep it as a wallpaper, then head home or let Hlid pick a pub nearby.

Nothing to unlock. One price.
Offline maps, the off-path buzz and every walk are included. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Early days
Walks start with day trips from south-west London, with more areas to come. If a walk sends you somewhere it shouldn't, let us know and we'll fix it.
